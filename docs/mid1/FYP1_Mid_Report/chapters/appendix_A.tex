\chapter{Proposal Action Register}

The FYP Committee approved the proposal for project F26-131-Halite on 14~September 2026 without any comments or mandatory changes. On 7~October 2026 it communicated the Proposal Defense result of 24.83/30. There are therefore no panel-ordered changes outstanding.

Three team-initiated clarifications were made to distinguish the approved project vision from the smaller FYP-I Mid implementation: (T1)~lexical bounded retrieval versus future semantic/hybrid retrieval; (T2)~118 aircraft instead of the approximate 60 initially planned; and (T3)~a real-data vertical slice rather than nine fully completed modules. Table~\ref{tab:panel-actions} in Chapter~3 records these clarifications and their supporting evidence. No panel action is represented as unaddressed.
